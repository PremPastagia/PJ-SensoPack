TrackingPacking-class IoT tags exceed the 5–8% of product value a shrimp pack can bear. Structurally absent from the informal market.

Fix 2 — unreadable

Needs special scanners

Senoptica-type optical labels need industrial readers — a consumer smartphone can't read them at retail.

Fix 3 — bolt-on

Not packaging-integrated

Blakbear-style add-on tags aren't built into the film — extra steps, extra cost. And "Best By" ignores temperature entirely.

The unserved opportunity: an affordable, integrated solution that both extends shrimp freshness and proves it — readable by the naked eye, deployable with no cold storage at all.
The Product

What SensoPack looks like in the customer's hands

Protect + Sense

Product visualisation (concept render — physical unit in development).

Protect

Active biodegradable film

Chitosan + essential oils (thymol/carvacrol/clove) — an active barrier that doubles shrimp shelf life and works without refrigeration.

Sense

One <₹5 colour indicator

An anthocyanin dye patch (butterfly pea / roselle) that shifts colour with spoilage amines — naked-eye readable, no electronics, optional NFC tap for organised buyers.

One decisive sensor, not a stack of five. Cheaper, more robust, genuinely affordable.
Architecture

An affordable base — with a full export-tier platform

How it works

Full-platform architecture (premium / export tier). The mass-market base tier is the film + ₹5 colour patch only.

Base tier · mass market

Active chitosan + EO film + a <₹5 anthocyanin colour patch — naked-eye, no electronics, no battery. What a wet-market vendor actually buys.

Premium / export tier · optional

Adds electronic amine sensing (MIP), an NFC digital freshness certificate, and a time-temperature log — for MPEDA exporters who need a cold-chain audit trail.

One decisive signal for everyone; electronics only where the value justifies it. We never force a five-sensor stack onto a ₹200 tray.
02 · Technical Feasibility & Soundness

Measuring the markers regulators already trust

Criterion 3

The four-stage shrimp spoilage cascade & accepted biomarker thresholds. Ref: Das & Mishra (2023).

Protect — established science

  • Chitosan + EO coatings extend chilled shrimp ~8 → 16 days (published)
  • Low OTR arrests oxidation & melanosis; balanced WVTR stops drip loss
  • Antimicrobials extend the microbial lag phase (stages 3–4)

Sense — the earliest reliable signal

  • We target stage-3 amines — TVB-N ≥30, TMA ≥3 mg N/100g
  • Anthocyanin dye shifts colour with these amines — correlated with TVB-N
  • Compensated read-out cancels humidity/temperature drift — the deep-tech edge
