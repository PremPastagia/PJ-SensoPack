Problem Significance

India exports the world's shrimp — and loses a fortune to spoilage

Biological Spoilage

Protein-rich foods (shrimp, fish, meat) hold high moisture & nutrients — a high susceptibility for rapid microbial growth and quality decay.

Unpredictable Cold Chains

Refrigeration slows decay, but unmonitored temperature breaches still cause loss. Conventional packaging protects — it cannot report freshness.

Originality & Creativity

Why current solutions fail — and our answer

The active-tag cost barrier: electronic tracking tags cost >$2.50/unit — unviable when the sensor must fit within 5–8% of package value.
Domestic reality: ~90% of the Indian food sector runs with no continuous refrigeration — visual point-of-sale checks are unreliable.

Dual-Function Smart Bioactive Film

Active antimicrobial protection and real-time freshness sensing in one biodegradable film — preserves food and reports its quality simultaneously.

Circular Bioeconomy Design

Turns shrimp-shell waste into a chitosan biofilm with natural essential oils, plus a reusable NFC tag — cutting plastic and e-waste.

Multi-Tier Democratic Platform

Scales from battery-free colour indicators for micro-vendors to AI-enabled NFC sensing for international exporters.

Technical Feasibility & Soundness

Detect · Sense · Manufacture at scale

Multi-Biomarker Detection
  • TMA — volatile amines of protein decay
  • TVB-N — broad freshness / shelf-life index
  • pH shift — real-time electrochemical tracking

Scientifically validated parameters track degradation across the full spoilage process.

Advanced Sensing Platform
  • MIP sensors — synthetic-antibody selectivity
  • 130 nm CMOS ASIC — clean digital signal, ultra-low power
  • NFC — passive, battery-free wireless reads

Molecular selectivity converted to stable freshness data, read with no battery.

Manufacturing Scale
  • Roll-to-roll / slot-die, high-volume
  • Ultra-thin film <20 µm
  • Fits existing heat-sealers — zero overhaul

Manufacturable on standard packaging lines with no factory overhaul.
