The unserved opportunity: an affordable solution that both extends freshness and proves it — for the vendor with no cold storage at all.
The Solution

SensoPack — one package, two functions

Protect + Sense
Protect

An active biodegradable film

Not a passive wrapper — an active barrier that limits oxygen, balances moisture, and releases natural antioxidant/antimicrobial agents to slow decay. Works without refrigeration.

Sense

One smart freshness indicator

A single sensor tracking the one decisive signal — the volatile amines of spoilage — read by colour (any user) or an NFC phone tap. A real verdict, not a printed guess.

Deliberate design decision: we sense one decisive signal, not a stack of four or five sensors. Fewer parts → lower cost, higher reliability, a product a vendor can actually afford.
Architecture

A rationalised, honest system — no over-engineering

How it works
Protect — material stack
Sense — signal chain (the deep-tech core)
We removed the sensor stack. The original four-to-five-sensor concept was over-built and unaffordable. One decisive sensor + one active film is cheaper, more robust, and genuinely deployable — and the novelty lives in the read-out, not a costly custom chip.
02 · Technical Soundness — Protect

The film is an active barrier, not a passive wall

Criterion 3
  • Low Oxygen Transmission Rate arrests lipid oxidation and myoglobin browning
  • Balanced Water-Vapour Transmission stops drip loss and microbial pooling
  • Phenolic hydroxyls scavenge free radicals at the interface
  • Released natural agents disrupt psychrotrophic spoilers
  • Chitosan bioactive films are a heavily-validated class — multi-day chilled shelf-life gains are well documented
We stand on established, reproducible science — our contribution is engineering it for cost and the tropical, unrefrigerated context.
02 · Technical Soundness — Sense

The deep-tech edge is the read-out, not the sensor

Criterion 3
  • Spoilage amines / TVB-N are the regulator-accepted freshness markers
  • Cheap gas sensors fail in cold, humid air — the real problem
  • Our novelty: a low-power signal-conditioning front-end with humidity + temperature compensation that keeps a cheap element reliable
  • Off-the-shelf parts — no custom chip, no battery
  • Squarely in the domain of analog / mixed-signal + resistive-MEMS sensing
Read-out front-end (schematic)

Ratiometric read against a reference cancels humidity/temperature drift — the core reliability trick.
