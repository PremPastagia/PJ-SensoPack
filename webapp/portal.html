Value Architecture

What the Client & User Gets From This Dashboard

Why this client dashboard is fundamentally different from a static sensor display. It turns raw sensor voltage and chemical reactions into actionable commercial decisions, insurance-backed audit logs, and verified profitability.

1. Early Biochemical Warning Before Visual Rot

Traditional inspections rely on subjective sensory checks (smell, touch, visual color) which only trigger when spoilage is irreversible. SensoPack detects volatile amine build-up (TVB-N/ammonia) and pH shifts at the microscopic onset, alerting managers up to 36 to 48 hours earlier to save cargo via re-icing or rapid local sale.

✓ Value: 94.7% Cargo Salvage Rate on Early Flags

2. End-to-End Cold-Chain Accountability

Eliminates disputes between seafood processors, reefer truckers, shipping liners, and supermarket buyers. If a cargo arrives spoiled at Rotterdam or Mumbai, the time-stamped inspection audit identifies exactly which transit leg experienced temperature abuse or cooling failure.

✓ Value: Zero Ambiguity on Transit Insurance & Rejection Claims

3. One-Click HACCP & FSSAI Export Compliance

Exporting seafood to the US FDA or EU markets requires rigorous proof of uninterrupted cold chain. The built-in database automatically creates standardized compliance logs that export directly to CSV or PDF, eliminating thousands of hours of manual clipboard logging.

✓ Value: 100% Digital Audit Trail for Customs & Food Safety Regulators

4. Direct Bottom-Line Financial ROI

A single 20-foot refrigerated container of Black Tiger Shrimp is valued between ₹40 Lakhs to ₹1.2 Crores ($50k–$150k USD). Preventing even one container rejection per quarter pays for the entire IoT sensor telemetry infrastructure for 3 full years.

✓ Value: Estimated 12x to 18x Annual ROI for Commercial Exporters
